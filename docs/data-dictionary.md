Inspected files are under `data/raw/raw/oulad/` in the current workspace (the pipeline also accepts `data/raw/oulad/`). No raw files were edited. CSV columns below are the actual headers. IDs are identifiers, not model features. Blank values are missing; `-` or other literal codes remain categorical values where present.

All seven inspected CSVs had zero duplicate full rows. This is not a substitute for validating every intended key; pipeline joins use explicit cardinality checks where applicable. The raw target distribution is Pass 12,361; Withdrawn 10,156; Fail 7,052; Distinction 3,024. For day-56 modeling, 6,053 withdrawals before the cutoff and 93 withdrawals with unknown unregistration timing are excluded because they cannot represent a reliably future event at prediction time. The model target combines Fail and Withdrawn on or after day 56; it is not a calibrated probability of future failure.

## Relationship outline

`studentInfo` ↔ `studentRegistration` on module, presentation, student. `studentInfo` ↔ `studentAssessment` through student ID and assessment context; `studentAssessment` ↔ `assessments` on assessment ID. `assessments` ↔ `courses` on module/presentation. `studentInfo` ↔ `studentVle` on module/presentation/student. `studentVle` ↔ `vle` on site/module/presentation. `courses` describes a module presentation and its duration.

## Feature opportunities and limits

From submissions, scores, due dates, and studentVle dates/clicks one can derive early assessment mean/count/on-time proportion, assessment coverage, click volume, active days/weeks/sites, activity types, and weekly click/day sequences. OULAD does not record physical attendance, login events, study intent, or learning quality. A VLE click is an interaction count, not attendance or proof of learning. Sensitive demographic fields are present in the source but excluded from model inputs; they may be considered separately in later fairness audits under appropriate governance.
